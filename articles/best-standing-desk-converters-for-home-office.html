What to Look for in a Standing Desk Converter

The most important factor is stability at maximum height. A converter that wobbles when you're typing defeats the purpose entirely. Look for dual-motor electric lifts or high-quality gas-spring systems. The work surface size should accommodate your monitor(s) and keyboard comfortably—the 32-inch and 36-inch options offer the most flexibility. Height range matters too; you want enough travel to accommodate both sitting and standing positions for your specific height. Finally, consider weight capacity—if you have heavy monitors, prioritize converters rated for 35+ pounds on the upper surface.
