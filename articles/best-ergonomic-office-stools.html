What to Look for in an Ergonomic Office Stool in 2025

Before diving into the reviews, here is what matters most based on my testing. First, adjustability is non-negotiable. Your stool should allow you to find a neutral hip angle where your feet can rest flat or on a footrest. In 2025, I'm seeing a rise in dynamic seating that encourages micro-movements to keep blood flowing. Second, pay attention to seat depth. If the seat is too deep, you'll lose lumbar support; too shallow, and you'll cut off circulation behind your knees. Finally, check the base stability. A five-star base with quality casters is essential for safety, especially if you lean back or shift weight frequently.
