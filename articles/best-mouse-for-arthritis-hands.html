After spending weeks testing ergonomic mice on my own arthritic hands, I’ve found that the right grip can mean the difference between a productive workday and painful wrist fatigue. Many standard mice force your hand into unnatural positions, aggravating conditions like carpal tunnel or rheumatoid arthritis, but specialized designs offer relief by supporting the natural curve of your palm. I prioritized mice with vertical orientations, thumb supports, and adjustable sensitivity to ensure comfort for users with limited dexterity. These selections are based on real-world usage, focusing on how they reduce strain during long hours of clicking and scrolling.
