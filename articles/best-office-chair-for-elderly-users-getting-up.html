Getting out of bed or rising from a seat becomes a daily negotiation for many of us as we age, making the right furniture essential for independence. I spent weeks testing six popular office chairs specifically to see how well they assisted users with limited mobility, focusing on seat height, armrest support, and cushion firmness. The difference between a chair that holds you down and one that lifts you up is night and day, so I narrowed down the field to the most reliable options on the market. Whether you need something for a home office or a dedicated reading nook, these picks offer the stability and ease of use that seniors truly need.
