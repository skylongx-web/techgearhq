How I Tested the Best Standing Desk Balance Boards

Testing balance boards isn't just about standing on them; it's about how they perform during real work scenarios. I tested each board for at least two weeks, alternating between work calls, coding sessions, and writing tasks. Key metrics included:

  • Stability vs. Challenge: Does the board rock smoothly without tipping, or is it too wobbly for typing?
  • Surface Grip: Does my foot slide off after sweating during a long session?
  • Footprint Size: Is it large enough to shift weight comfortably, or too small to be effective?
  • Noise Level: Does it creak or click against hardwood and carpet?
