After spending countless hours color-grading 4K footage and staring at timelines until my eyes burned, I learned that a monitor is only half the battle when it comes to physical comfort. The other half is how that monitor interacts with your neck, shoulders, and spine throughout those marathon editing sessions. I tested several high-end displays specifically looking for ergonomic flexibility, color accuracy, and screen real estate that doesn’t demand you crane your neck to see the details. What I found was that the best monitor for video editors with neck pain isn't just about resolution; it's about height adjustment, tilt, and screen curvature that reduces eye strain and keeps your posture neutral.
