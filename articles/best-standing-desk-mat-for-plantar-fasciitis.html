After spending dozens of hours on my feet managing a home office, I know that standing all day isn’t always the cure for back pain—it can often make foot issues like plantar fasciitis worse if you aren’t supported correctly. I tested several mats against the hard concrete of my garage workshop and the polished floors of my living room office to find the ones that actually save your arches. After weeks of testing, I found that thickness, material density, and surface texture are the three non-negotiable factors for pain relief.
