Best Monitor Arms for Ultrawide Monitors in 2025: Expert Reviews & Top Picks

After spending months wrangling everything from 34-inch curved beasts to massive 49-inch super ultrawides, I've learned that width changes everything. Standard monitor arms often sag, tip over, or fail to accommodate the lateral bulk of an ultrawide display. The center of gravity shifts further out, requiring arms with robust weight capacities and superior clearance. In this guide, I'm breaking down the arms that actually handle the width and weight without turning your desk into a wobbly mess. Whether you're running a single massive screen or a dual ultrawide array, these are the arms that survived my torture test and earned their spot on your desk in 2025.

What to Look For in an Ultrawide Monitor Arm

Before diving into the reviews, here's what I found matters most when upgrading to an ultrawide setup:

  • Weight Capacity: Ultrawides are heavy. Ensure the arm handles not just the monitor weight but the leverage factor caused by the extended depth.
  • Width Clearance: If you're going dual, check the minimum separation distance. Some arms can't get close enough for side-by-side ultrawides.
  • Gas Spring vs. Mechanical: For monitors over 27 inches, gas spring arms offer smoother motion and better hold over time, reducing sag.
  • Cable Management: Ultrawides often have thick power bricks and multiple cables. Arms with integrated routing channels are essential for a clean look.
