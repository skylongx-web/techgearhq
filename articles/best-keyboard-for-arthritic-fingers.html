After spending months battling morning stiffness and chronic pain in my knuckles, I’ve learned that finding the right ergonomic keyboard is not just a luxury—it’s a necessity for daily comfort. I tested over a dozen models, ranging from split mechanical boards to compact low-profile options, to see which ones truly alleviate pressure without sacrificing typing speed. What I found was that the "best" keyboard depends heavily on whether you prefer a split design to open up your shoulders or a tented layout to keep your wrists neutral. Below are my top six picks for arthritic fingers, based on my real-world testing and comfort levels.
