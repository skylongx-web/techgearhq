Why a Monitor Light Bar Changes Everything

Before diving into the products, here's why you should ditch the desk lamp. Monitor light bars use asymmetric optical design to shine light downward onto your desk while keeping the light off your screen. This eliminates screen glare, reduces eye strain, and creates a focused workspace without illuminating the dark corners of your room. Plus, in 2025, many bars now feature USB-C power delivery, meaning you can power your laptop while you work, keeping your desk cable-free.

How I Tested These Bars
