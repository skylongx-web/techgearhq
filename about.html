About TechGear HQ

TechGear HQ is a small, independent review site about home-office gear. We write for people who spend eight hours a day at a desk and want their body to still work at the end of it.

What we cover

Standing desks, ergonomic chairs, monitor arms, keyboards, mice, lighting and the small accessories that make a workspace usable. Our focus is gear that solves a specific problem — wrist pain, a bad back, a tiny apartment, a night shift — rather than whatever is trending.

How we choose what to recommend

  • We read the manufacturer specs and compare them against the actual complaint the product is meant to solve.
  • We weigh verified owner feedback, especially complaints that repeat across hundreds of reviews.
  • We list a real downside for every pick. If a product has no trade-offs, we haven't looked hard enough.
  • We do not accept payment for a placement or a ranking.

How we make money

TechGear HQ is reader-supported. When you buy through links on our site, we may earn an affiliate commission — at no extra cost to you. These commissions fund the site and keep it free to read.

TechGear HQ is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.com. As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases.

Commissions never influence which products we recommend or how we rank them. If a product is not worth your money, we say so.

Corrections

Gear changes, prices change, and we get things wrong sometimes. If you spot an error or a product that no longer deserves a recommendation, let us know through the contact details published on this site and we will fix it.
